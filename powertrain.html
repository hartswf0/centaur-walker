Why the threaded rod unscrews itself

A nut that touches something stationary while the rod turns gets dragged backward relative to the rod. On one side of each part that drag unscrews the nut; on the other side it screws it tighter and binds. A single nut on a turning threaded rod always walks. Two nuts tightened hard against each other (a jam pair) cannot turn relative to the rod, so they ride with it.
