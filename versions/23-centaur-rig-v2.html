Centaur Rig

One pair of hind legs on one crankshaft, one leg per side. They sit on your bike's rear rack and turn off the back tire, or hang from a backpack and turn off your heels. Every pin is a hole through flat plates. The three benches share one clock and one set of bar lengths.
